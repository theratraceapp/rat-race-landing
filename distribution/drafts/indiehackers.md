## Intro post

**Title:** Validating a %-to-goal money tracker with a kill-gate: here's my landing page, what would you change?

**Body:**

I'm building (or maybe not building) Rat Race — a net-worth tracker organized around your trajectory to a goal date instead of a frozen balance. The thesis: wealth is a rate, not a balance, and "a number with a date is a plan." Four leagues from $0–$1M to $100M+, solo-first, progress measured as % toward your own goals rather than raw-wealth comparison.

The twist: I'm refusing to write code until the market votes. 4-week validation sprint, landing page + waitlist only at theratrace.app. 1,000 emails = build. 300–1,000 = extend or reposition. Under 300 = kill or pivot, publicly.

Why I'm here: I want the landing page torn apart by people who've done this before. Specifically:

1. Is the value prop legible in under 10 seconds?
2. What's the highest-friction element on the page?
3. Is a waitlist signup real validation or am I measuring politeness?

I'll post honest numbers at each milestone — including if I kill it. Week [N]: [X] signups.

## Product page description (idea stage)

**Tagline:** A rat race you can win. The MMO for money.

**Description:** Rat Race is a gamified net-worth tracker built around your trajectory to a goal date — not your balance. Pick a goal and a date; the product is the line bending toward it. Four leagues (The Climb $0–$1M through The Dynasty $100M+), solo-first, and progress is measured as % toward your own goals, never raw wealth against anyone else.

**Status:** Validating. Landing page + waitlist only — 1,000 signups in 4 weeks greenlights the build, under 300 kills it. Follow the sprint: theratrace.app
