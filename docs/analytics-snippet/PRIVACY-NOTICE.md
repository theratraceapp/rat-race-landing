# Privacy notice — analytics paragraph

Paste this (adapted to your footer/privacy page) on the landing page.
Because the analytics setup is cookieless, this disclosure replaces a
consent banner — no banner is shown.

---

**Analytics.** We measure anonymous, aggregate usage of this site with
Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It does not use cookies, does not
fingerprint your device, and does not collect personal information. What we
see: which pages are visited, where visitors came from (referrer / campaign),
approximate country, and device type — plus anonymous counts of interactions
like waitlist signups and outbound link clicks. If your browser sends a
Do-Not-Track signal, you are excluded entirely. Survey responses and the
email you share with us are handled separately by our survey provider and
are never sent to our analytics.

Analytics data is hosted in the United States.
