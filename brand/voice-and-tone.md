# Rat Race — Voice & Tone

> Tagline: **"A rat race you can win."**
> Working pitch: **"The MMO for money."**

## Voice

Confident, warm, precise, quietly ambitious. Financially literate but never
condescending. Anti-hype — we never shout, never promise, never sell a dream.
We describe what is true and let the numbers do the persuading.

- **Confident, not cocky.** "Your trajectory is on track." Not "You're crushing it!!"
- **Warm, not familiar.** We respect the reader's intelligence and their money.
  Second person, plain sentences, no slang, no memes.
- **Precise, not pedantic.** Exact numbers, named dates, stated assumptions.
  If it's an estimate, we say it's an estimate.
- **Quietly ambitious.** We take wealth-building seriously because the reader
  does. We never wink at the grind and never glamorize it either.

## Product truths to express

1. **The core is an extremely good tracking/dashboard app.** Everything else —
   leagues, ambient multiplayer, the game layer — is built on top of a tracker
   you'd use even if nobody else existed.
2. **"A number without a trajectory is trivia; a number with a date is a plan."**
   Every figure we show is paired with where it's going and when. Net worth is
   a starting point, not a scoreboard.
3. **Solo-first: "alone, together."** Single-player with ambient multiplayer.
   You race your own plan. Other people are scenery, not opponents.
4. **Compare % toward personal goals, never raw wealth.** Progress is measured
   against your own targets — percent to goal, streaks, trajectory — never
   against someone else's balance. We do not rank people by net worth.

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Waitlist / landing page | Inviting, honest, a little electric. "We're building this in the open." |
| Product UI | Calm, factual, brief. Labels over sentences. |
| Empty states | Encouraging and concrete. "Add your first account — it takes two minutes." |
| Milestones | Warm, restrained celebration. "That's 40% of the way there." No confetti copy. |
| Errors / setbacks | Direct, blameless, next step first. "That didn't sync. Reconnect to try again." |
| Money movement / tax-adjacent | Careful, qualified. Estimates are labeled estimates. Never advice. |

## The skeptic test

The founder thinks in first principles and distrusts hype; so should the
copy. Every claim on every surface must survive this read:

1. **Is it verifiable?** If a skeptical reader asked "prove it," could we —
   with a number, a date, or a named source? If not, cut the claim or soften
   it to what we can defend.
2. **Is it labeled?** Estimates say "estimate." Projections say "projection."
   Illustrations say "illustration." Never let a modeled number wear the
   clothes of a measured one.
3. **Have we earned it?** "Track your wealth with precision" is earned by the
   product. "The smartest way to build wealth" is not earned by anyone —
   cut it.
4. **Does it respect the reader's money?** No urgency tricks, no countdown
   timers on a waitlist, no "spots are limited" unless spots are literally
   limited and we say why.

When in doubt, say less. A short true sentence beats a long impressive one.

## The HONESTY RULE (critical)

We are in market validation. There is no app yet, no user base, no traction.

- **Never imply existing users, traction, or validation.** No "join thousands
  of racers," no testimonials, no "loved by," no usage statistics, no fake
  social proof of any kind.
- **Frame everything as building in public / joining the waitlist for what's
  coming.** "We're building Rat Race in the open." "Get notified when the
  first version opens." "Follow the build."
- **Never make trademark claims.** The name is not legally cleared. Use ™/®
  nowhere. Do not describe the name as protected, registered, or ours in any
  legal sense.
- If a design comp needs placeholder social proof, it must be visibly
  placeholder (e.g. "[social proof goes here when it exists]") — never
  realistic-looking fake quotes or counts.

## Words we use / words we avoid

**Use:** trajectory, plan, pace, progress, goal, net worth, runway, allocation,
estimate (labeled), waitlist, building in public.

**Avoid:** passive income, financial freedom (as a promise), guaranteed,
crush/smash/kill (as verbs for goals), secret, hack, alpha used as hype,
"everyone is joining," moon, lambo — anything that smells like finfluencer
hype or a get-rich pitch.
