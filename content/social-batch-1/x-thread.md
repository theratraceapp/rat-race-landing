**Post 2**
Every wealth app shows you the same thing: a number. Frozen. Static. $214,380 — and nothing about where it's going.

**Post 3**
But wealth is a rate, not a balance. Two people with $100k: one saving $5k a month, one spending $2k more than they earn. Same number. Opposite futures.

**Post 4**
The real question isn't "what am I worth?" It's "what am I worth by 2030?" Draw the line from here to there — that line is the whole game.

**Post 5**
That's the idea behind Rat Race: a wealth tracker built around your trajectory — where you're headed, by when, at your current pace.

**Post 6**
Along the way, you pass through leagues. The Climb ($0–1M). The Freedom ($1–10M). The Empire ($10–100M). The Dynasty ($100M+). Milestones on the map, not status symbols.

**Post 7**
Alone, together: your money is yours, but the journey doesn't have to be solo. Pace yourself against anonymized peers in your league. Motivation without comparison poison.
