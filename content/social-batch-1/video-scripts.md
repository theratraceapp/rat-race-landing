# Video Scripts — TikTok / Reels / Shorts (Batch 1)
**Format:** 30–60s each | **Style:** Talking head, teleprompter | **Brand:** dark background, gold accents in on-screen text
**Rules:** Hook in first 2 seconds. No financial advice, no specific investment recommendations. No claims of users/traction. CTA: follow + waitlist (theratrace.app).

---

## Script 1 — "The trajectory line" (~45s)

**HOOK (0–2s):**
On-screen text: "Your net worth is trivia."
Spoken: "Your net worth number is trivia."

**BODY (teleprompter):**
"Every finance app shows you the same thing — a frozen number. Two hundred fourteen thousand dollars. And nothing about where it's going.

But wealth is a rate, not a balance. Two people with a hundred K: one saves five thousand a month, one spends two thousand more than they earn. Same number. Opposite futures.

The real question isn't 'what am I worth.' It's 'what am I worth by twenty-thirty.' Draw the line from here to there — that line is the whole game."

**ON-SCREEN CUES:**
- 0:00 — "Your net worth is trivia." (big, struck-through style on "trivia")
- 0:12 — "$100k" splits into two lines: one rising "+$5k/mo", one falling "−$2k/mo"
- 0:30 — "What am I worth by 2030?" with a line drawing toward a date

**CTA (last 5s):**
Spoken: "We're building Rat Race — a tracker built around your trajectory. Follow along, link in bio."
On-screen: "🐀 theratrace.app — a rat race you can win."

## Script 2 — "The leagues" (~45s)

**HOOK (0–2s):**
On-screen text: "Wealth has 4 chapters."
Spoken: "Wealth has four chapters. Which one are you in?"

**BODY (teleprompter):**
"Chapter one: The Climb — zero to one million. The proving ground. Every dollar is earned twice: once by making it, once by not spending it.

Chapter two: The Freedom — one to ten million. Work becomes optional.

Chapter three: The Empire — ten to a hundred million. Wealth becomes a machine.

Chapter four: The Dynasty — a hundred million plus. The question stops being 'how much' and becomes 'for whom, and for how long.'

A goal thirty years away feels abstract. But four finishable chapters? You always know which chapter you're in — and what the next one takes."

**ON-SCREEN CUES:**
- 0:00 — "4 CHAPTERS" with four badge icons fading in
- Each chapter: badge visual + range ("$0 → $1M" etc.) in its accent color
- 0:38 — trajectory line with four chapter markers

**CTA (last 5s):**
Spoken: "We're building this in public — follow to see it take shape. Waitlist is in the bio."
On-screen: "🐀 theratrace.app"

## Script 3 — "Alone, together" (~40s)

**HOOK (0–2s):**
On-screen text: "Your money is yours. The journey doesn't have to be."
Spoken: "Here's a weird truth about building wealth: it's lonely."

**BODY (teleprompter):**
"You can't talk about money at dinner. Your friends don't know your number, and you don't know theirs. So you grind in silence, with no idea if your pace is good or terrible.

We call our answer 'alone, together.' Your money stays yours — private, always. But you can pace yourself against anonymized people in your league. Same chapter, similar trajectory.

Motivation without comparison poison. A leaderboard where the only person you're really racing is last month's you."

**ON-SCREEN CUES:**
- 0:00 — "Building wealth is lonely."
- 0:15 — "ALONE, TOGETHER" title card
- 0:25 — anonymized league leaderboard mock (no real data — labeled "concept")

**CTA (last 5s):**
Spoken: "Rat Race. A rat race you can win. Follow the build — waitlist in bio."
On-screen: "🐀 theratrace.app"

**Production notes (all three):**
- Film in a dim room, warm key light; dark-first grade to match brand (#0A0A0B backgrounds, #C9A15C text accents).
- Captions burned in, Inter-style clean sans; key phrases in Fraunces-style serif gold.
- Keep jump cuts tight; each script reads at ~150 wpm for teleprompter timing.
